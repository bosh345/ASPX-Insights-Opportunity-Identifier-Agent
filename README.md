# ASPX-Insights-Opportunity-Identifier-Agent
Built for sellers who need to land value, expand usage, and drive secure AI adoption at scale.
Copilot Opportunity Agent
AI-driven sales productivity focused on turning fragmented signals into clear, actionable next steps for sellers.
Overview
Most sellers are not constrained by opportunity, they are constrained by time and signal.
With a significant portion of effort spent on administrative work and manual interpretation of data across multiple systems, opportunity identification becomes inconsistent and slow. The Copilot Opportunity Agent addresses this by bringing together telemetry, insights, and account context into a single pipeline that prioritises where sellers should focus and what action to take next.
This shifts sales from reactive account management to proactive, insight-led execution at scale.

What this solves

Fragmented data across dashboards, CRM, and telemetry
Manual account prioritisation and low signal quality
Missed revenue opportunities due to delayed intervention
Inconsistent execution across large portfolios

Outcome:
Enable sellers to consistently act on the highest-value opportunities in real time

How it works
The Copilot Opportunity Agent integrates live telemetry and growth insights to:

Score each account based on adoption signals
Identify the next best action
Deliver prioritised recommendations to sellers

This replaces instinct-led selling with evidence-led execution.

Adoption propensity model
Each account is scored against four key signals to determine the next best action:
1. Copilot Upsell — Expanding healthy adoption

Strong active usage
Available licensed capacity
Clear expansion opportunity

2. Convert unlicensed to paid — Monetising demand

High free Copilot usage
Low paid conversion
Proven demand with monetisation opportunity

3. Unhealthy adoption — Protecting revenue

Paid investment not translating to usage
Low engagement patterns
Requires enablement and intervention

4. Agent 365 readiness — Expanding beyond productivity

High Copilot maturity
Active agent usage
Ready for workflow automation scenarios
